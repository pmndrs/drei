> **Using WebGPU?** `Sky` works on both renderers: it wraps three's `Sky` on WebGL and `SkyMesh` on WebGPU. For a more realistic sky, look at [`@pmndrs/sky`](https://github.com/pmndrs/sky). It is a physically based sky and atmosphere for the WebGPU renderer, driven by real solar position, with aerial-perspective haze, a star field and R3F bindings. See the [docs](https://sky.docs.pmnd.rs).
